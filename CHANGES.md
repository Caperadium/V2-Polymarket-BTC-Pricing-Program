- Fix stranded inventory: risk-controller pulls no longer remove the unwind path. Rule (c) INV_CAP now emits the one-sided AWAY mode (long -> ASK_ONLY, short -> BID_ONLY) at ANY breach ratio -- the >1.5x "extreme" escalation to PULLED is removed (it protected nothing: a one-sided-away mode never adds risk, and the reduce leg stays bounded by sizing's reduce-side floor and inventory headroom caps; meanwhile q_max = q_max_scale*S'(x) shrinking under a fixed position as p drifts to a wing made the escalation a permanent inventory trap -- observed live on the VPS: 58000 short on all three ladders PULLED for hours with no exit until settlement). Rule (f) LIQ_DEGENERATE now emits the reduce-only side instead of PULLED when the market holds inventory (new optional `RiskController.evaluate(inventory_q=...)` kwarg threaded from a per-tick harness inventory snapshot; None/flat preserves PULLED, so un-threaded callers are unchanged). Feed-dead, near-resolution, staleness, vol-shock and manual pulls deliberately unchanged. Observability: heartbeat gains top-level `stranded_markets`/`stranded_shares` (current-tick; markets holding inventory whose quotes are PULLED outside the near-resolution window) plus a per-expiry `stranded` count, computed in `paper_runner._expiries_heartbeat_dict`; mm_monitor shows a STRANDED tag per expiry and two new top metrics; deliberately no alert. Tests: extreme-breach tests repurposed to one-sided semantics (risk controller, integration, harness ws1), new rule-(f)/co-fire/harness-wiring/heartbeat tests.
